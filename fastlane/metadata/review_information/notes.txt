The app requests camera access only when the user opens Cargo Hatch (Scan hatch from the harbour) and taps Open the hatch camera. The usage description string is: MacroDock reads barcodes to check cargo into the pantry manifest. There is no login, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content shared with other users, no account system or account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. MacroDock is a personal food and macro tracker for people who want to log what they eat, plan meals, and track pantry stock at home. Nutrition figures come from Open Food Facts; the app states it is not medical advice. On a cold launch the app shows a brief loading state while it contacts https://macrodock.pro/api/v1/users/register with the bundle identifier and an empty push token. If a content URL was previously cached or returned by that call, the app may display a full-screen web view; otherwise the native harbour interface appears within five seconds. No credentials or demo account are needed. On first launch, first-watch onboarding appears as a full-screen sheet with pages READ THE HOLD, SEARCH OR SCAN, SET THE TIDE, and HOLD TARGETS. Tap Skip with defaults or Next watch through the pages, then Weigh anchor to reach the harbour home. From there use Search quay or Scan hatch to find a product, open Lading to set grams and Confirm berth, and return to Today's Tide. Manifest, Voyage plan, Wish pennants, Hold stock, and Tide goals are available from the dock controls. Tide Goals includes Contact the dock office, which opens https://macrodock.pro/contact-us in a sheet web view. External services contacted are Open Food Facts at world.openfoodfacts.org for product search and barcode lookup, macrodock.pro for the launch registration endpoint and contact page, and Open Food Facts image URLs when loading product thumbnails. Food logs, targets, cached products, wish pennants, pantry stock, and restock indents are stored locally in SQLite on the device. The app contains no hardcoded regional behaviour; whether the launch registration returns a web content URL depends on the server response, not on device locale. No gambling, healthcare-provider, or other regulated-industry licence applies; MacroDock is a consumer food log and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.
